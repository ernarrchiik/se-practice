# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

6 to 8 stories. Keep the shape exactly: ID, the As/I want/so that sentence, a priority, one
assumption. Roles are **Student** or **Administrator** only.


---

### US-01 — View availability
**Story:** As a student, I want to see which study rooms are available at a selected time, so that I can choose a room for studying.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** The student selects a date and time to check available rooms.

### US-02 — Book room
**Story:** As a student, I want to book an available study room for a time slot, so that I have a room reserved for studying.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A booking must satisfy rules R1–R4.

### US-03 — Cancel booking
**Story:** As a student, I want to cancel my booking, so that other students can book the released time slot.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** The student has an existing booking to cancel.

### US-04 — Receive confirmation
**Story:** As a student, I want to receive confirmation when I book or cancel a room, so that I know my action was completed.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** The system sends confirmation after a successful booking or cancellation.

### US-05 — Review room usage
**Story:** As an administrator, I want to see how the rooms were used over a selected period, so that I can understand their usage.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** The administrator selects a period to review.

### US-06 — Block or unblock room
**Story:** As an administrator, I want to block or unblock a study room, so that students can book only rooms that are in service.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A blocked room cannot be booked (R4).
